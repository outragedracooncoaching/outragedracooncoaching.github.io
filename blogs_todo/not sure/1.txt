Title: [Dunno, help me out Claude]

When I was on holiday I decided to just sit with my iPad and draw whatever came to mind. No reference image, no goal, no judgement. 

No derpy pigeons or frogs 🙈

Harder to do than it sounds, especially with an adult brain used to logic and structure!

It was super fun! I started with the bold, raw teal and red landscapes. Then the minimal mountains. Finished with some creepy trees which I’ll share later on Cara because they are fun but don’t fit this set 😂

When was the last time you let yourself create something with zero goals attached?

P.S I know! No racoons! Don’t worry, more are coming 🫡
