Title: Escape The Derpy Dungeon is Live on Itch!

Fourteen months ago I started drawing a derpy pigeon tarot card. Because why wouldn't you.

Today there are 53 derpy monsters attached to poker cards, living in a dungeon that one poor field archivist has to keep on top of.

His name is Boz. He has been down there fourteen weeks. He was told it would take four days.

Escape the Derpy Dungeon is out.

It's a solo card game. Play it with a normal deck of cards, or print and build the 53 hand-drawn creatures yourself. There's a craft guide for making your deck look like it survived something.

Every line of it is mine. Drawn by hand, laid out by hand, playtested until the brutality of the game became the design.

Free rules and a free colouring sheet if you want to poke at it. No pressure. 

[The pigeon will find you either way.](https://outragedracoongames.itch.io/escape-the-derpy-dungeon)
