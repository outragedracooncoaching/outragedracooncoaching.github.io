Title: I drew a tiny truck then I added cats (titlecase this please Claude)
Date: 3 months ago
I drew a tiny truck this week. Then I added cats. No regrets.

When was the last time you did something just for the joy of it?

I wasn't trying to get better.
I wasn't making content.
I wasn't planning to sell anything.

In fact, I wasn't even going to post it.
But your feed needed a break from AI and I'm here for you.

I just thought the truck was cute and wanted to draw it 🥰.

Somewhere along the way, a lot of us stopped doing things just because.

❌ Everything has to be productive.
❌ Or profitable.
❌ Or impressive.

And that's a shame, because curiosity has a funny way of spilling over into the rest of your life.

These seemingly pointless little acts of creativity help me:
Stay kind.
Stay rested.
Stay calm on the days that go to sh*t.

So what's your tiny truck? 🚚
