What happens next

  1. Email me with the rough shape of what is going on. A sentence or two is fine.
  2. I reply. We work out whether we are a good fit and which format makes sense. If it is not right, I will say so.
  3. Agree format and payment before anything is booked. The Decisive Hour, The Sort-It Sequence or In Your Corner: whichever fits what is actually happening. Screen-free phone sessions are available if you'd rather be away from your desk.
  4. We get to work. Remote 1:1, no preamble. The Sort-It Sequence is spaced roughly every two weeks — close enough to keep momentum, spread enough to let things move.
